Built for students, not stress

Most productivity apps feel like a coach yelling from the sideline. Quiet is more like a calm friend who remembers your due dates, notices when you drift, and says “hey, don’t forget” — not a motivational speech.

Never miss an assignment again. Gentle reminders before things are due. Focus sessions when you need to actually sit down. Short recaps when you're done.

Focused During your timer — steady and present.
Reminder Before a due date — calm, not panic.
Celebrating When you finish — quiet wins count.
Encouraging When something's due soon — you've got time.
Thinking Reading your syllabus scan — finding dates.
Rest Between sessions — breaks are allowed.

Distraction isn't failure. Return is the practice.

What Quiet does

  • Reminds you early — 24h, 6h, and 1h before assignments are due, with friendly one-line messages.
  • Runs focus sessions — pick a task, set 25–60 minutes, log distractions without shame.
  • Recaps honestly — focused time, drift count, and a short coach note. No lecture.
  • Scans syllabi — upload a PDF or photo; Quiet pulls due dates for you to confirm.

What Quiet doesn't do

No guilt streaks. No “productivity score” that makes you feel behind. No long AI essays in your notifications. Quiet stays small, calm, and useful — like a good study buddy should.
