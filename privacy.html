Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026. This policy explains what Quiet Focus collects and why, in plain language.

Who we are

Quiet Focus (“we,” “us”) is a student focus timer and assignment reminder app available at quietfocusai.com. Questions: contact support.

What we collect

What we collect depends on how you use the app:

  • Account info (optional): If you sign in, we store your email and basic profile info from Google or email/password signup (via Supabase Auth).
  • Focus sessions: Task name, session length, distractions, breaks, and recap stats — only if you sign in and use cloud sync.
  • Assignments: Titles, courses, due dates, and reminder status you add or confirm after scanning.
  • Push notifications: If you enable reminders, your browser generates a push subscription endpoint stored so we can send due-date alerts.
  • Waitlist: Email address if you join the waitlist on our website.
  • Assignment scans: When you use AI scan while signed in, extracted text from your upload (not the original PDF/image file) is sent to our server and then to Google Gemini to pull out dates and task names. We trim this to about 2,500 characters of relevant lines.
  • Local data (guest mode): If you use the demo without signing in, assignments and sessions may stay on your device only (browser storage).
  • Technical logs: Our hosting and database providers may log IP addresses, device type, and error data for security and reliability.

How we use your information

  • Run the focus timer, assignment list, and reminders you ask for
  • Sync your data across devices when you sign in
  • Send optional push or in-app reminders before due dates
  • Extract assignment details from scanned documents (AI scan, signed-in users only)
  • Improve reliability, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests
  • Email waitlist subscribers when the mobile app is ready (one-time updates, no spam)

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising.
  • We do not upload your full PDF or photo to AI — only extracted text you already see in the scan preview.
  • Reminders use preset friendly messages; we do not send your assignments to an AI to write notification text.

Third-party services

We use trusted providers to run the app:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and edge functions
  • Google — optional sign-in (OAuth) and Gemini AI for assignment scan extraction
  • Google Analytics — page visits and basic usage stats (measurement ID G-R9S0676XRS)
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymous heatmaps and session replays to improve the site (no ads)
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Browser push services — Apple, Google, or Mozilla, depending on your device, to deliver notifications you opted into

Each provider has its own privacy policy. We only share the minimum data needed for them to provide their service.

Your choices

  • Guest mode: Use the app without an account; data stays local on your device.
  • Notifications: Optional. You can decline permission or disable reminders in the app.
  • AI scan: Optional. Local date parsing works without AI. AI scan requires sign-in.
  • Delete data: Email us to request account or data deletion. You can also clear site data in your browser settings.

Children

Quiet Focus is built for students. If you are under 13, please use the app only with a parent or guardian’s permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent.

Security & retention

We use industry-standard hosting and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is 100% secure. We keep your data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, then delete or anonymize it when you ask or when it is no longer required.

International users

Our servers and providers may be in the United States. By using Quiet Focus, you understand your information may be processed there.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app grows. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Use our support form for privacy questions or deletion requests.
