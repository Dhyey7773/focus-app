How it works

Four steps. No setup tour.

Open the demo, pick a duration, start. Add assignments when you need reminders.

1

Write the task. Pick 25, 45, or 60 minutes.

One line for what you're doing. The timer starts immediately — no accounts, no config screens.

2

Drift, notice, return.

When you catch yourself off-task, tap "I'm distracted." When you're back, tap again. The coach stays brief — one sentence, no chat thread.

3

Finish and read the recap.

See focused time, distraction count, a timeline of the session, and a short insight. Sign in if you want sessions synced across devices.

4

Scan syllabus → get reminders.

Upload a PDF, photo, or .txt from the Tasks tab. Quiet pulls due dates, lets you add them in one tap, and nudges you before deadlines.
